# Rate Limiting

## What is Rate Limiting?

Rate Limiting restricts how many requests a client can make within a specific time.

## Why Important?

- Prevent Abuse
- Avoid DDoS
- Fair Usage

## Common Algorithms

### Token Bucket
Requests consume tokens.

### Leaky Bucket
Processes requests at a fixed rate.
